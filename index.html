Rooted in the harvest,
driven by innovation

SucroCane was founded in 1994 with a single purpose: to turn every part of the sugarcane plant into high-value derivatives that benefit people and the planet. Today we operate three world-class processing facilities and partner with more than 1,200 growers across tropical regions.

Our vertically integrated model ensures traceability from soil to shipment, giving customers the confidence of certified, consistent quality.

  • ISO 9001:2015 & ISO 22000 certified
  • Non-GMO verified supply chain
  • Rainforest Alliance partnership
  • Zero liquid discharge processing plants

Our product portfolio

Six major derivative categories — each rigorously tested and available in bulk, food-grade, and pharmaceutical-grade specifications.

Food & Beverage

Refined & Raw Sugar

ICUMSA 45, 100, and 150 grades; VHP raw sugar for direct consumption and industrial sweetening.

Energy

Bioethanol

Anhydrous and hydrated fuel-grade ethanol meeting ASTM D4806 and EN 15376 standards.

Feed & Fermentation

Molasses

High-Brix blackstrap and final molasses for animal feed, fermentation, and yeast production.

Biomass & Energy

Bagasse & Biopower

Pressed bagasse pellets for co-generation; excess electricity exported to the national grid.

Specialty Chemicals

Sugarcane Wax

Naturally extracted octacosanol-rich wax for cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, and food coatings.

Agriculture

Vinasse & Filter Cake

Nutrient-dense organic by-products recycled as bio-fertilisers to close the crop cycle.
